# Revamp suite decision: research brief

DECISION: Recommend launch, bounded test and defer product formats using the recovered October 23 research and subsequent explicit creative decisions. No catalog mutation.

CHANGE CONDITIONS: A feasible US supplier, credible use/purchase evidence and adequate modeled delivered contribution can promote a candidate. Poor image fidelity, incompatible printing, thin shipping economics or excessive creation effort demotes it. Historical assortment alone is not a reason to retain it.

BOUNDARIES: US gifts from dogs to people. Mug, blanket, framed/unframed wall art, ornament, pillow, compact book and calendar. Preserve word-free blanket, meaningful portrait, shared styles/tone and sample-last sequence. No broad novelty expansion without evidence.

FRESHNESS: Read current connected supplier bases and official US product/shipping pages October 24. Preserve the October 23 demand report as historical evidence, without treating its seller reviews as representative US demand. Prices and availability remain snapshots.

QUALITY FLOOR: Direct supplier specifications and current account reads for feasibility; transaction-linked evidence from the recovered report for category signals, with its limitations retained. Separate availability, US route, production speed, delivery and quality reputation. No sample means physical quality is unverified.

TIME/COST CAP: Bounded read-only research pass using connected tools and public primary pages. No paid scrape, order, account signup, publication or generation.

STOP RULE: Each serious candidate has an explicit disposition, supporting and contrary evidence, supplier status, margin scenario or maximum allowable delivered cost, and the smallest unresolved test. Stop before collecting more weak reviews that cannot alter the call.
