## The offer in one breath

Heartside paints your dog from one photo onto a mug, a blanket or a portrait, as a gift from the dog to the person it loves. A short note in the dog's own voice comes in the box and as a free digital card. Free US shipping. Order by December 4 for Christmas.

Lead line: **"Your dog loves you too."** Christmas line: **"This year, the dog's buying."**

## How it works (the buyer's two minutes)

1. Add one clear photo of the dog on heartside.io.
2. Pick a look (style) and a feel (tone). The tones run from sentimental to Roast, where the dog teases the person it loves. Answer a few taps about the dog, such as where it rules the house.
3. The painter paints the dog in about a minute. The buyer sees the painting on the gift before adding it to the cart.
4. A person checks every order before it prints. Printful prints and ships it.

## Promises we can make (each one is backed by a live policy or a live system)

- **Free standard shipping** on every US order, to US addresses only.
- **Timing:** printing usually takes 2 to 5 business days, then delivery takes 3 to 7. These are typical times, not guarantees.
- **Christmas:** order by December 4 for delivery by Christmas to a US address. At the slowest times, an order placed on December 4 arrives by December 22.
- **Something wrong:** if a gift arrives misprinted, damaged or wrong, tell us within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We refund in full, shipping included, or remake it free. Nothing needs to be sent back.
- **Cancellation:** a full refund until the order goes to print. Personalized pieces can't be returned for a change of mind after printing.
- **Tracking by email:** each parcel's tracking goes out when it ships. Since 10 November, every Printful shipment marks the Shopify order fulfilled and sends Shopify's shipping email with tracking (`n8n/MARK-FULFILLED.md`).
- **Their own dog everywhere:** every order email shows the buyer's own painting, not a stock dog. The order status page adds "Your painting" once the order-painting app is installed (`docs/review/COWORK-ORDER-PAINTING.md`).
- **Help from a real person:** the help chat in the corner of every page answers questions at once. Anyone can ask for a person, and someone from the team replies by email within 6 hours (`n8n/HELP-CHAT.md`).

## Angles that are true and sell

- **It sounds like their dog.** The note is written from the owner's own answers and the breed's personality, so a Border Collie and a Poodle don't sound alike. The story behind it is a blind model test and Krish's ruling that Claude writes the note (`NOW.md`).
- **Roast.** A tone where the dog teases the person it loves, with affection: who throws the ball badly, who steals the couch. It is shareable and funny, and it is never cruel.
- **The gift from the dog.** People already sign cards "from the dog", and Heartside makes that real for partners, parents, best friends and dog moms and dads.
- **Fast and easy on a phone.** One photo, a few taps, and the painting is ready in about a minute. You see it before you pay.
- **A person checks every order.** The AI never has the last word: a human reviews each design before it prints.
- **Christmas is sorted** if they order by December 4, with free shipping.

## Claims we must not make (yet)

- **Reviews, star ratings and customer counts.** Heartside has no published customer reviews yet, and one paid order so far (Krish's own #1013, delivered on 8 November). Real testimonials are allowed only as each person's own words, with their consent, a real photo or initials, and a label for how they experienced it, such as "Tried the gift maker" or "Gifted". This follows the FTC's 2024 rule on reviews and testimonials, and `docs/SCOPE.md`. Never invent, reword or AI-generate a review or a reviewer's face.
- **Physical quality.** Don't claim physical quality, durability or "gallery quality" until the sample order has been inspected (`NOW.md`). We can say "printed and shipped by Printful".
- **Delivery dates as guarantees.** Typical times only, as listed above. The Christmas date is "order by December 4".
- **Words on the gifts.** None are printed, and the note comes in the box.
- **Countries outside the US.** We ship to US addresses only.
- **"Handmade" or "hand-painted".** The painting is made by the painter, an AI artwork system, and checked by a person. The Artist's touch is the only hand rework.
