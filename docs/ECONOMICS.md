# Pricing for maximum total profit with a30% net floor

## Current owner ruling, October26

Maximize expected total net operating profit, subject to a 30% net operating margin floor after all attributable business costs. The October26 owner ruling supersedes the mandatory50% floor; supplier-only margin is not net margin. Retrieve actual costs or use explicit conservative bounds without restarting the deferred labor interview. Exact price/discount activation, publication, spending and product acceptance remain separately gated.

The configured baseline is USD45 mug, USD59 unframed portrait, USD119 framed portrait and USD129 blanket; [`../NOW.md`](../NOW.md) owns the current price readback. Proposed offers are inactive. An existing qualified US shipping discount is separate from those offers; do not generalize one quoted destination to every US address. The [pricing ruling](evidence/2026-10-26-pricing-ruling.md) owns the decision. The superseded 50% supplier-cost model and scenario CSV are in [`history/ECONOMICS-2026-10-24.md`](history/ECONOMICS-2026-10-24.md) and [`history/pricing-scenarios-2026-10-24.csv`](history/pricing-scenarios-2026-10-24.csv).

For any proposed price or offer change, model expected net revenue after discounts/refunds, then subtract product, shipping, taxes borne by the business, processing, artwork/retries, app allocation, support, remake/return and acquisition costs. Use actual supplier/account quotes and observed order mix where available, conservative ranges otherwise. Compare **total** expected net operating profit while requiring modeled aggregate net operating profit divided by net revenue to remain at least 30%. Record volume and mix sensitivity; a single product's supplier gross margin is insufficient. Reconcile actual attributable costs after fulfilled orders before certifying realized net margin.
