## 5. Phone test (iPhone 13 emulation, preview theme)

- The five steps work. Tapping a chip moves to the next step on its own after a short delay. If a shopper taps a chip and then Next quickly, a step is skipped (it happened in testing).
- "Approve and order Gerald's poster" opens `/products/the-annual-review?dog=Gerald&person=…&cupboard=…&improvement=…&incident=…&enemy=…`.
- On `?view=review`, the answers card says "Copied into the personalizer below. Change anything you like there." All six Teeinblue fields were filled correctly, and "Use Gerald's headshot" opened Teeinblue's cropper.
- `?view=landing` works the same way.
- **Bug for Claude Code:** if the shopper leaves step 1 untouched, the bridge copies the placeholder name "Sarah" into "Employee name (you)", so the poster would print Sarah.
- Cosmetic issues:
  - The Teeinblue block shows a second price, and the theme shows a variant dropdown labelled "Available Product / Color / Size".
  - Product descriptions are Printful's stock text.
  - A two-line "Area for improvement" sits close to the INCIDENT REPORT heading on the print.
