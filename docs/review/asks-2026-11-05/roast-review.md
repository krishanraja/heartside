The same wording is in the v18 shipping prompts and in design/examples-2026-10-29-breeds/README.md:57.   - Fix: Make a person-out-of-frame candidate the default: R2 (the camera is the person, which matches 'judging its person, who stays out of frame'), R3, or a new one. Present R1 to Krish explicitly as overturning his 4 November ruling, quoting SCOPE.md:43. If he does overturn it, record it as 'Ruling (Krish, YYYY-MM-DD): ...'. Rewrite the Roast bed and feet place texts without the person, or hold them until that ruling.
- **major.** At the send (hs2.js:400) set hs_brief_input.frozen = true, so a late answer never changes the fields and gifts 2 and 3 reuse the same staging.
  - Problem: journeyAutoSend is only one of five send paths. journeyPick (hs2.js:326, the Teeinblue picker on 'nophoto' or 'refused'), journeyOverride (hs2.js:428), journeyResume (after journeyOrphan) and journeyRepaint all send without passing line 400. Separately, the hold condition `j.briefHold && !spot && elapsed < 20 s` never clears briefHold when the cap runs out unanswered, so gift 2 would hold for another 20 s.
  - Fix: Freeze where every send passes. Either set frozen inside blanketUploadSent()/beginBlanketUpload(), or have journeyBrief refuse whenever sessionStorage holds journeySentKey(j) (journeyPick sets it to 'picked:...'), cityArtSaved() is true, or the page is not the first gift. Clear briefHold at the first send of any kind, and only ever hold on queue index 0. Add browser tests for the nophoto-then-pick-then-spot case and for gift 2 after an unanswered cap.
- **major.** Cost of (b1): the photo goes about 2 to 5 s later than today, inferred from the 5 November logs (send 3 to 7 s after the page opened) and a local question time of about 6 s; the hold caps at 20 s.

The claim that buyers 'only feel it if they finish all nine screens first' is unmeasured: the logs show the reveal times, not when the questions ended.

Measure on a draft theme with no photo sent: the page open to the first question showing, and the painter-open time. Quote a range, not 2 to 5 s.
