Mostly sound and well grounded, with one blocker and several majors to fix before it is built. What I verified: the file:line references hold (bundleLine hs2.js:2972-3009, artistTouch 3015-3089, journey() 309-319 with 6 h, hs-journey-choose.js:14-17/41-44/344, handOver 282-302, ?gift= start 949-955, cart.json product list disabled, all_products in hs-footer 48-50, hs-cart-quantity gate 141/213). I re-ran the colleague's probe (/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-heartside/2e077d0e-1ec9-5845-8fab-a9e6b1438927/scratchpad/probe-extend.mjs, local only). It reproduces: an appended queue entry paints once (__painted 1; the second file change event is hs2's own prepare-and-redispatch, not a second send), shows "gift 2 of 2", asks nothing, and handles no photo, a stale 7 h journey and the White frame variant as reported. Live GETs agree: /cart carries data-hs-bundle-live and data-deadline="2026-12-04" and has no cart-drawer-component, / has one, and the live product options and prices match. The extend design (append to hs_journey.queue through hs2's in-memory state, never change t) is the right core idea. The flaws are at the edges. (1) The "start" fallback repaints the gift already in the cart. I verified this in the preview. (2) The tap-outside-lands rule can hit Remove on a painted gift. (3) Replacing a queue entry in place sends the buyer on a detour. (4) The slot and the auto-open hurt the primary Check out path on small phones. (5) The default portrait recommendation steers buyers to the weakest-margin basket, and the margin is quoted wrong. Krish has no approved taste rules for Heartside yet (get_approved_taste returned 0), so that adds nothing. My probe scripts: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-heartside/2e077d0e-1ec9-5845-8fab-a9e6b1438927/scratchpad/review-probe/start-stale.mjs, /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-heartside/2e077d0e-1ec9-5845-8fab-a9e6b1438927/scratchpad/review-probe/checkout-pos.mjs, /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-heartside/2e077d0e-1ec9-5845-8fab-a9e6b1438927/scratchpad/review-probe/probe-redo.mjs.

## Flaws

- **blocker.** Start mode: the link /?step=photo&gift=<gift> 'starts a new journey: photo, style, feel, then gifts with that gift ticked. The existing cart line keeps its design.'
  - Problem: hs-journey.js:951-952 only adds the wanted gift: `if (!S.gifts[w]) S.gifts[w] = {on:true, variant:null}`. It never clears the gifts the earlier journey ticked. sessionStorage 'hs-journey' (S) lives for the whole tab, so a same-tab buyer whose journey is over 6 h old still has the mug ticked. I verified this in Chromium against out/index-journey.html: with S.gifts={mug:on} and a /?step=photo&gift=blanket visit, S.gifts became {mug:on, blanket:on}. GIFT_ORDER is print, mug, blanket, so 'Start painting' queues and paints the mug first. That is a second paid painting of a gift already in the cart, and it invites a duplicate mug, when the buyer tapped 'Paint the blanket'. The same state also has S.noteDone=true, so HSJourney.done() is true and the new first gift page asks nothing. The claim 'its gift page asks the questions while it paints' is therefore false in this case.
  - Fix: The drawer's start link must carry what is in the cart: /?step=photo&gift=blanket&have=mug,print (or &from=cart). In hs-journey.js init's `q.get('gift')` branch, when that parameter is present, set S.gifts[g].on=false for every gift in `have`, so the gifts screen shows them as 'In your cart' or hides them. Optionally pass &variant= through for the wanted gift. Test, failing today: preset hs-journey with gifts {mug:{on:true}}, hasPhoto, noteDone; open /?step=photo&gift=blanket&have=mug; assert sessionStorage hs-journey .gifts has only blanket on, and that handOver's queue (or the gifts screen's ticks) is [blanket].
- **major.** No scrim; 'A tap outside the drawer closes it, and the tap still lands.'
  - Problem: On phones the drawer is min(360px, 100vw-48px) wide, so at 375 px its edge is at x=327. In the preview cart at 375x667 the line's Remove button ('Remove Your Dog's Illustrated Mug ...') sits at x 296-340, y 335, and the title link ends at 327. I measured this with /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-heartside/2e077d0e-1ec9-5845-8fab-a9e6b1438927/scratchpad/review-probe/checkout-pos.mjs. Helio's real CSS is not in the repo, so the live geometry is inferred. A dismiss tap in the 48 px strip can therefore remove a painted, personalized gift or step its quantity, which starts the quantity gate. That breaks the purchase path at its most valuable point.
  - Fix: Swallow the dismissing tap. On pointerdown in the capture phase outside the drawer, close the drawer, then preventDefault and stopPropagation on the following click (or put a transparent catcher over the uncovered strip while it is open). Test: open the drawer on a one-gift cart at 375x667, tap (335, Remove's centre y), and assert that the drawer closed, the row is still in the cart, and no /cart/change request was made.
- **major.** journeyMore 'replaces an existing entry by handle or adds a new one'; after the gift page adds, nextGift() or /cart.
  - Problem: nextGift() is queue[at()+1] (hs-journey-choose.js:48, 343-344). If an entry is replaced in place, any later queue entries remain. Take a gift that was queued on the home page but skipped or removed, for example queue [mug, blanket, print] with the mug and portrait in the cart: re-adding the blanket from the drawer sends the buyer to the portrait page, which is already in the cart. It shows 'in your cart' and needs another tap before /cart. This breaks 'one step at a time, non-duplicative'. I found this by reading the code; I did not run it.
  - Fix: In journeyMore, remove any existing entry for the handle and push the new one to the end. Better still, in hs-journey-choose.js add(), use the cart() result already read there and skip queue entries whose handle already has a line in the cart. Test: queue [mug, blanket, print], cart has mug and portrait, extend with the blanket, add, and assert it lands on /cart.
- **major.** Auto-open 1.2 s after hs2's first cart pass, after 700 ms idle; on phones it covers the page's Check out, which is mitigated by the drawer's own Check out; focus stays where it was.
  - Problem: On the live store the scripts load late. The 5 Nov judges recorded questions arriving 11 s after the page opened (hs-journey-choose.js:23-25). The 'first cart pass + 1.2 s' can therefore land 5 to 12 s in, just when the buyer is reaching for Check out. On phones the drawer covers Check out and the express buttons (shopify-accelerated-checkout-cart), and the drawer's own footer has no Shop Pay or Apple Pay. Because the drawer is non-modal while covering the whole page on phones, keyboard and screen-reader focus can sit on or move to elements hidden behind it. That fails WCAG 2.2 SC 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured. An auto-open while focus is on Check out hides the focused control.
  - Fix: Limit the auto-open to an early window (for example, open only if it can open within about 3 s of first paint, otherwise show only the opener). Never auto-open if focus or hover is inside .cart-totals or the summary, or after any pointerdown. On widths where it covers the page, open it as a modal: aria-modal=true, the page made inert, focus trapped and returned. Or show only a small 'peek' tab that bounces out from the left and opens fully on tap, which still answers Krish's 'bounces out from the left'. Put this choice to Krish.
- **major.** The slot after </table> in cart-products.liquid is server-rendered 'so neither Check out nor the totals move'; cart-layout test: 'Check out's position stays the same before and after the slot and drawer appear'.
  - Problem: Server rendering prevents a late shift, but it moves Check out down compared with today. In the preview at 375x667, Check out is at y 538-592 today. At 320x568 it is at 541-595, already partly below the fold. A slot of at least 64 px plus margins pushes it about 70 to 80 px lower, below the first screen on an iPhone SE once Safari's toolbars are counted. The proposed test compares two states that both contain the slot, so it can never catch this.
  - Fix: Reuse the place the cart already keeps. The bundle line's ::before has a reserved min-height of 40px in .cart-totals__tax-note (hs2.css:1276-1280). Render the opener there as a real button ('Add a second gift and save 15%. See the gifts'), or put the slot after the express buttons. Test against a recorded baseline: the y of [name=checkout] on today's one-gift cart at 320x568 and 375x667 must not increase.
- **major.** Decision 4: default portrait Unframed $59 (recommended); 'mug plus Unframed portrait at 15% passes by only about $3.70 to $3.98'.
  - Problem: docs/evidence/2026-10-27-bundle-economics.md:68 shows mug + unframed at 15% as +8.29 / +3.98 / +8.00 / +3.70 / -0.48 FAIL. It fails the 30% floor in the bounding case, which the report leaves out. The same note (line 310) says a mug added to a framed portrait or a blanket clears the floor in every stack. Defaulting to Unframed for a mug-first cart steers the drawer's traffic to the thinnest basket, against ECONOMICS.md and the North Star's margin condition.
  - Fix: State the bounding-case failure in the decision. For a cart that contains the mug, recommend leading with the blanket ($26.10 saving) and defaulting the portrait to Framed. Otherwise lead with Unframed and let Krish choose.
- **minor.** Motion: translateX(-100%) to 0 in 520 ms with cubic-bezier(.3,1.35,.55,1) overshoot.
  - Problem: An overshoot on a panel anchored to the left edge carries it past x=0, so a strip of page shows at the left edge at the top of the bounce. On phones that is a visible gap.
  - Fix: Give the panel a left bleed (left:-24px; padding-left:24px, background continuous), or put the overshoot on an inner element while the shell eases without overshoot.
- **minor.** Card button 'disabled after the first tap', then location.href = url.
  - Problem: Back from the gift page restores /cart from the browser's page cache (Safari, Firefox), and the button stays disabled. Nothing listens for pageshow. hs-journey-choose.js:461-466 handles this case for its own button.
  - Fix: On pageshow with e.persisted, re-enable the buttons, recompute offers from the rows, and apply the 'opens once more' rule. Test with page.goBack() in Chromium with bfcache, or by dispatching a synthetic pageshow.
- **minor.** Units come from each row's data-hs2-cart-line; saving = bundleOff(units + chosen variant).
  - Problem: A redo pair (a gift and its new version, _hs_redo_of, shown as 'Keep the new one?') counts as two units. The drawer would then say 'Third gift: 20% off all three' and a saving the buyer loses once they remove the first version. The report also defines no pill copy for a cart already at 3 or more units (for example 3 mugs), or with 0 gift units (only add-on or orphan lines).
  - Fix: Count a redo pair as one gift, and do not auto-open while any 'First version' prompt is showing. Define copy for 0, 1, 2 and 3 or more units, or hide the pill when no tier step is left.
- **minor.** bundleOff 'matches the go-live readback: [4500,12900,5900] gives 4660'; rounding 'per unit. This follows the go-live record, step 7.'
  - Problem: docs/evidence/2026-10-27-go-live.md step 7 read back only $26.10 (mug + blanket) and $43.80 (two mugs + blanket). $46.60 is computed, not read back. The record says nothing about per-unit rounding. With whole-dollar prices, 15% and 20% always land on whole cents, so rounding never matters.
  - Fix: Label 4660 as computed and add it to the live QA cart list. Drop the rounding claim, or say it does not matter at today's prices.
- **minor.** 'extend without photo' happens 'after PHOTO_DAYS = 3 (hs2.js:1125)'.
  - Problem: Extend requires a journey under 6 h old, and the photo is saved when that journey starts, so the 3-day expiry essentially cannot cause it. The real causes are a private window, an IndexedDB failure, or a photo replaced since.
  - Fix: Remove PHOTO_DAYS from the list of causes.
- **minor.** Slot: server label 'Add another gift' that JS changes to 'Paint one more from the same photo' in extend mode.
  - Problem: Swapping to a longer label after first paint can wrap to a second line at 320 px, which changes the slot's height. That breaks 'nothing changes size'.
  - Fix: Use one label that fits every mode ('Add another gift') and change only text inside a reserved line, or reserve two lines from first paint.
- **minor.** Drawer Check out 'clicks the page's own [name="checkout"]... If there is no such button, it goes to /checkout.'
  - Problem: Setting location to /checkout skips hs-cart-quantity's blockCheckout, which only intercepts click, auxclick and submit events. While a change is pending the page's button is disabled and inert, so the click silently does nothing. The drawer's own Check out is not said to reflect the pending state.
  - Fix: Disable the drawer's Check out with the same words while HSCartQuantity.pending(). For the fallback, click a real <a href="/checkout"> so the gate still applies.
- **minor.** Analytics events hs_more_open, hs_more_pick, hs_more_close, hs_more_pay.
  - Problem: The theme's convention is hs2_<name> through track() (hs2.js:4008). The funnel pixel forwards only the names in its EVENTS list (shopify/pixels/hs2-funnel.js). Nothing ties a resulting order to the drawer either, so its effect on net sales, the North Star, cannot be measured.
  - Fix: Use track('more_open') and so on, giving hs2_more_*, and add them to the pixel's EVENTS list if wanted. Carry a hidden line property (for example _hs_from=cart_more) on the queue entry into hsProps() so orders can be attributed. Do not record buyer names.
- **minor.** Test plan: run on out/cart.html, and add cart-two.html and cart-three.html to render.mjs.
  - Problem: The existing cart tests render cart-products.liquid with liquidjs (tools/preview/cart-fixture.mjs: renderCart, gift, addon), with hs2's cart functions sliced in. out/cart.html differs from live: it contains Helio's <cart-drawer-component auto-open> (live /cart has none), data-deadline="", and no data-hs-bundle-live.
  - Fix: Build the 1-, 2- and 3-gift carts with cart-fixture.mjs renderCart and set the memo attributes in the fixture. Keep out/cart.html only for a smoke test, and assert that cartGifts ignores #cart-drawer rows.
- **minor.** Pictures: home scene photos recommended because 'the blanket's variant photo is still the old scatter pattern.'
  - Problem: hs-ex-scene-blanket-home.webp is itself a scatter of small black Labradors (I viewed it), and PROMPT-SPEC v19 says the blanket keeps its scattered figures. The stated reason does not hold. The home photos may still be the right choice, for consistency with the gifts screen.
  - Fix: Base the recommendation on consistency with the approved home gifts screen. Use the mug colors in hs-journey.liquid's brand order (Black, Dark Blue, Dark Green, Red, Pink), not the HEX object's order, and state the default mug color.

## Missing

- A pre-existing bug that the drawer widens: the cart's 'Not quite right? Make another version.' link (/products/<h>?variant=..&redo=<id>) for a journey gift opens in journey mode. hs2-journey-on:<pid> equals j.t and the gift is in the queue, so the panel shows "Mowgli's mug is in your cart" and hides the redo. I verified this in the preview with probe-redo.mjs. Every gift added to the queue gets the same behavior. journey() should stand down when ?redo= is present.
- The buyer's own photo (restorePhoto File through an object URL) or the cart line's own painting as proof of 'the same photo', next to the Example product photo. It is cheap and makes the offer personal.
- Reassuring copy: say the current gift stays in the cart, for example 'Your mug stays in your cart.'. 'The saving comes off at checkout' is also inaccurate, because Shopify's automatic discount already shows in the cart after the add; 'It comes off by itself. No code needed.' is more accurate.
- A queued gift that was already painted but not added: its page goes through the existing 'The painting stopped' path, which repaints on a tap, so 'Most paintings take 1 to 3 minutes' is the wrong promise for it. Either detect the sent key and use different copy, or warn that it paints again.
- Interplay with Helio's phone menu drawer on /cart (heartsideMenu), which may also open from the left (inferred): close this drawer when the menu opens. Also: overscroll-behavior: contain on the drawer's list, 100dvh or inset sizing for iOS toolbars, and touch-action: pan-y for the swipe-to-close.
- Unavailable states: a sold-out gift or variant, or all_products returning nil. The drawer and slot must hide that card without collapsing the layout.
- The Christmas card offer: every new gift line also offers a card (cardOffers). The drawer should not compete with or duplicate it. Say whether the drawer mentions it.
- Tests that would fail today for the fixes above: the start link unticks gifts already in the cart (verified to fail now); an outside tap never activates Remove; extending a replaced entry still lands on /cart; Check out's y position does not regress against today's baseline at 320x568 and 375x667.
