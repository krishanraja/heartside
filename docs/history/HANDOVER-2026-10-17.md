## Product implementation for the sharing experiment

Growth owns the acquisition hypothesis, public creative and campaign measurement. This repository owns any changes to `hs3.js`, the first-party link destination and the customer's sharing choice.

The existing code already offers a static card on the choose-gifts screen beside the cart action. A proposed test moves a lightweight option to the reveal and lets the buyer keep the gift private or share a moment. Avoid spoiling the recipient's gift. A shared link can invite another household to create its own gift with editable suggestions. The normal gift and checkout path stays usable without sharing or signing up.

Start with the existing card and ordinary links. Do not require generated video per visitor. Use opaque referral IDs with no names or private answers in URLs; personalized content becomes shareable only through the buyer's explicit choice. Verify share, save and copy fallbacks in real in-app browsers. Any new storage or processing must fit the existing Shopify and n8n architecture and the approved privacy policy.

Return to growth a documented event contract: share intent, link opened, referred creation started/completed and confirmed purchase attribution where technically available. Do not claim that opening a share dialog proves a send, or that rendering the card proves a share.
