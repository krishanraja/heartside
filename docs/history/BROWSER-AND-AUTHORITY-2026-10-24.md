If cross-chat browser attachment is unavailable, explain that specific limitation and ask Krish to select the existing task tab. Do not claim to have opened it or create an alternative remote session.
8. After actions, read fresh page state before the next dependent click. Prefer observed semantic controls. If a click navigates unexpectedly or does nothing, inspect before retrying.
9. Keep routine research in the background. At stage end, close temporary tabs. Preserve only the critical active tabs with documented handoff controls where available.
10. Record the purpose and URL of each retained tab in the current checkpoint, not as permanent IDs. Credentials, cookies and browser tokens never enter this corpus.
