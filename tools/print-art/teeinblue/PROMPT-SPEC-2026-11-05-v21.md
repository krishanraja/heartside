## Why

Thirteen real paintings on 5 November showed that a situation alone does not read as Roast at a glance. Krish first proposed lettering on the Roast mug, then cancelled it the same day: "It's going to add way too many more variables that might go wrong." Instead: "maybe winking or like a cheeky grin, something a bit more anthropomorphic." Not smug: v18's smug side-eye rebuilt the face and cost the likeness on 4 November.

Four test paintings followed through Teeinblue's own painter on the live store, on Krish's Border Collie's mug, on the cushion throne scene: two with a wink, two with a cheeky grin.
- **The wink:** visible in both paintings, with the face, ears, blaze, bandana and build unchanged.
- **The grin:** looked the same as no change, because his photo already grins.
- **Krish's pick:** "Wink 1 is good."

## What changed

1. **Every Roast text winks** on the mug, the portrait and the card, for every place and for no answer:
   - The mood opens "Mood: a loving roast, told by the situation and a playful wink."
   - The dog is "looking straight at the camera with one eye closed in a playful wink".
   - The couch text (and the no-answer text) is byte for byte the one that painted Wink 1.
   - The bed and feet texts are v20's with the same wink line added; these are untested in paint.
2. **Roast has its own face rule and identity line** (`brief.likeness.faces.over_the_top`):
   - **Face rule:** "The face is the dog in the photo: the same head shape, ears, eye color, muzzle, markings and coat; only the expression is playful. Never grumpy, mean, smug or exaggerated."
   - **Identity line:** the v19 face lock with "the expression" added to what may change.
3. **Unchanged:** Sentimental, Warm and Hilarious keep the calm face and v20's texts. The blanket's figures keep v20's text and the calm face lock.
