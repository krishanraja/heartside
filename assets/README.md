# Heartside brand assets

Three files supplied by Krish, and two built from them. Use the supplied files as given. Do not redraw, retype or recolour them.

| File | Size | What it is | How to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `heartside-logo.png` | 1296 x 438, transparent | The wordmark: "heartside" in a high-contrast display serif, a watercolour heart behind the letters "rt", and "EST. 2022" beneath on the right | Site header, email header, video end card. Keep clear space equal to the height of the letter "h" on all sides. Minimum width 120 px on screen. On dark photography, place on a cream or blush panel; do not invert. |
| `heartside-heart-watercolor.png` | 431 x 391, transparent | The watercolour heart alone, in blush, rose and peach | Favicon, loading pulse, section divider, "thank you" mark. One heart at a time. Do not tile or use as a texture. |
| `hero-dachshund.png` | 2000 x 1310 | A chestnut-and-tan dachshund resting its chin on the floor, looking up at the camera | Homepage hero and the emotional reference for all photography and video. Source and licence not recorded; confirm that Krish can use it commercially before launch, or replace it with owned or licensed imagery. |

| `favicon-512.png` | 512 x 512, transparent | Built from the watercolour heart by `tools/build_brand_assets.py` | Shopify favicon (Online Store > Themes > Customize > Theme settings > Favicon). |
| `social-share-1200x628.png` | 1200 x 628 | Built by `tools/build_brand_assets.py`: wordmark, "Gifts for people whose dog is their person.", and the ornament | Shopify social sharing image (Online Store > Preferences). It carries no offer or date on purpose, because platforms cache it for weeks. Uses the hero dachshund, so it waits on that licence too. |

## Colour values (sampled from the logo and heart)

Blush `#F0C0C0`, rose `#E49CB4`, peach `#F0B4A8`, ink `#121010`. Page background cream `#FBF6F1`. Warm accent chestnut `#8A4A2B`. Full rules are in `docs/BRIEF.md` section 7.
