## Shared standard

- The uploaded photo is the identity authority. Preserve markings, face shape, coat, ears and visible anatomy. A breed match is insufficient.
- Use actual Buddy answer facts for actions and objects. Never infer personality from breed, generated gift copy or shipping address.
- Buyer effort stays at the existing conversation and photo. Do not introduce a design questionnaire or several text boxes.
- A quiet dog and sparse answers must still produce a complete, restrained portrait with no invented habits.
- Exact words are a separate production layer; image-generated text is concept-only.
- Product-specific composition must work at its real print size and around its physical constraints.
- Artwork approval does not establish native source resolution, final print quality, automatic fulfillment or owner-confirmed likeness.
