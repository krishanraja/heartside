Order by December 4 for Christmas delivery to a US address. The strip at the top of heartside.io counts down the days, and each product page shows its own delivery estimate before you pay.

- **The painting.** Your dog is painted from the photo you upload, and you review the artwork before it goes in your cart.

Telling Buddy about your dog takes about two minutes, and one good photo is enough. The illustrated mug is $45, the sherpa blanket $129, and the 12 × 18 inch portrait $59 unframed or $119 framed. ## What do you get a dog lover who has everything?

**Short answer:** Give them their own dog, painted. A mug, a blanket or a portrait painted from a photo of their dog is one thing they cannot already own, and a short note in the dog's voice comes in the box.

---

People who have everything still have one thing nobody else has: their dog. A gift built around that dog is personal from the start.

### Pick the gift for how they spend time with the dog

- **An illustrated mug ($45)** for the morning walk and the coffee after it.
- **A sherpa blanket ($129)** for the couch the dog has claimed.
- **A 12 × 18 inch portrait**, $59 unframed or $119 in a black, white or red oak frame, for a wall they pass every day.

### Let the dog do the talking

The note in the box is written in the dog's voice, from what you tell Buddy about the dog's habits. It names real things: the spot on the bed, the sound of the car, the toy that goes everywhere. It ends with the dog's name.

### Find a good photo

Use a clear, recent photo where the dog's face is easy to see. Their own posts and messages are often the best place to find one.

### Related questions

- **Do the gifts have words printed on them?** No. Each gift is the painting only. The note comes in the box and on a free digital card.
- **Can I see the painting first?** Yes. You review the artwork before it goes in your cart.


## What can grandparents get from the grandkids' dog?

**Short answer:** A portrait or a mug painted from the dog's photo, with a note from the dog in the box. Grandparents who miss the dog between visits see it every day, and the note gives the family a line to laugh about at the next visit.

Grandparents often fall for the family dog as hard as for the grandkids. A gift from the dog keeps the dog in their house between visits.

### The gifts that work best

- **A portrait**, 12 × 18 inches, $59 unframed or $119 framed, for the hallway or the living room.
- **An illustrated mug ($45)** for their morning coffee.
- **A sherpa blanket ($129)** for the chair the dog always takes when it visits.

### Let the grandkids help

Buddy asks about the dog with tap-to-answer questions, so children can choose the answers. The note comes out in the dog's voice and ends with the dog's name.

### Ship it to them

Enter the grandparents' address at checkout. Shipping is free in the US, and for Christmas, order by December 4.

### Related questions

- **Can the note speak to the grandparents?** Yes. The note is written to the person you name when you tell Buddy about your dog.
- **What if the dog has a long name?** Use the name the family says out loud. A nickname works well.
