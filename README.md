# Heartside

Heartside sells personalized gifts from a customer's dog, hosted by Buddy. The current US core is an 11 oz illustrated mug, a word-free 50 × 60 sherpa blanket, and a 12 × 18 portrait sold unframed or framed. The mug and portrait can carry a short note and signature. The configured retail baseline is $45 mug, $129 blanket, $59 unframed portrait and $119 framed portrait. These are storefront prices, not proof of realized net margin. The ornament is held, the pillow is a conditional pilot and the book is planned for 2027. The lead line is "Your dog loves you too." The measurable [North Star](docs/NORTH_STAR.md) is $50,000 Shopify net sales over the trailing four weeks by 22 February 2027; its $10,000 ordinary-month phrase is part of the owner's broader ambition.
