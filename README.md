# Heartside

Heartside sells gifts from your dog: a mug, a sherpa blanket, a framed print and a tree ornament carrying a short message from the dog to the person it loves, in the dog's own voice ("I took your side of the bed. Not sorry.", signed "Love, [dog]"), drawn from the buyer's answers in a chat with Biscuit, our AI dog host, and made to order by Printful for US customers at heartside.io. The lead line is "Your dog loves you too." and the display name on every platform is "Heartside: Personalized Dog Gifts". Launch is Sunday 18 October 2026, and the goal is $10,000 in sales by Christmas.
